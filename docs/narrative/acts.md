# Narrative: Acts Breakdown

---

## Act I — The Consequence

- **Protagonist:** Past Self
- **Setting:** Sector 0 Chrono-Facility (Hours prior to initial breach)
- **Narrative Objective:** Investigate unusual temporal telemetry, identify an impending reality collapse, and decide how to respond to cryptic emergency messages signed with personal biometric signatures.
- **Major Events:**
  - Emergency alarms trigger in the temporal containment core.
  - Telemetry reveals an irreversible timeline breach forming in the future.
  - A secure terminal displays incoming transmissions demanding the immediate execution of a lockdown protocol.
- **Major Choices:**
  - *Choice 1.1:* Execute the authoritarian lockdown protocol immediately, trapping colleagues inside the containment ring, OR delay lockdown to evacuate personnel.
  - *Choice 1.2:* Conceal the transmission from the governing council OR broadcast the alert across the facility network.
- **Consequences:**
  - Sets initial `Self-Trust` and `Timeline Stability` baselines.
  - Sets persistent flags: `terminal_accessed`, `evacuation_attempted`, `council_notified`.
- **Reveals:** The biometric cipher confirming that the incoming warnings originate from the protagonist's own future self.
- **Transition:** As the breach detonates, the perspective shifts forward across decades to the aftermath.

---

## Act II — The First Interference

- **Protagonist:** Future Self
- **Setting:** The Remnant Citadel (Decades post-collapse)
- **Narrative Objective:** Enforce temporal containment over the surviving sectors, suppress temporal rebellions, and compensate for the causal shockwaves caused by Act I decisions.
- **Major Events:**
  - Causal waves from Act I strike the Citadel, causing structural matter dissolution in refugee sectors.
  - A faction of timeline dissidents attempts to sabotage the stabilization pylons.
- **Major Choices:**
  - *Choice 2.1:* Suppress the dissidents with lethal temporal force to secure the pylons, OR negotiate, risking a catastrophic drop in stability.
  - *Choice 2.2:* Divert limited temporal shielding to protect civilian habitats OR reinforce the Citadel's core calculation grid.
- **Consequences:**
  - Substantially mutates `Tyrant Index` and tests `Timeline Stability`.
  - Sets persistent flags: `dissidents_purged`, `core_reinforced`, `refugee_sector_lost`.
- **Reveals:** The Future Self maintains mathematical telemetry proving that mercy accelerates the universe's descent into entropy.
- **Transition:** The Future Self accesses historical records, pulling the player's focus back to the Past Self attempting a correction.

---

## Act III — The Correction

- **Protagonist:** Past Self
- **Setting:** Sub-orbital Relay Station & Chrono-Vaults
- **Narrative Objective:** Infiltrate the high-security temporal vaults to retrieve the Aion Compass and attempt an aggressive timeline intervention to overwrite the Future Self's regime.
- **Major Events:**
  - Confrontation with security defense systems and temporal shadows of past loops.
  - Discovery of the classified vault containing ancient timeline relics.
  - Recovery of the **Codex of Regression**.
- **Major Choices:**
  - *Choice 3.1:* Claim the Chrono Blade to sever the future causal links OR claim the Aion Compass to navigate the divergence lines safely.
  - *Choice 3.2:* Destroy the facility's transmission array to prevent future communications OR establish a two-way temporal uplink.
- **Consequences:**
  - Adds `chrono_blade` or `aion_compass` to inventory.
  - Significant shifts to `Self-Trust` and `Timeline Stability`.
- **Reveals:** The Codex of Regression reveals that this timeline loop is iteration #4,112. Every previous iteration that rejected tyranny collapsed entirely.
- **Transition:** The shock of the realization triggers a temporal resonance that pulls perspective into the Future Self's foundational memories.

---

## Act IV — The Origin

- **Protagonist:** Future Self
- **Setting:** The Nexus Spire (During the historical apex of The Catastrophe)
- **Narrative Objective:** Relive the pivotal historical tragedy where the protagonist originally made the conscious choice to abandon moral idealism and institute the Tyrant Doctrine.
- **Major Events:**
  - A containment breach threatens to dissolve the entire continent.
  - The young protagonist attempts to rescue survivors, inadvertently causing a cascading collapse that destroys millions.
- **Major Choices:**
  - *Choice 4.1:* Commit the cold mathematical execution of the infected sector to preserve the world, OR hesitate in an attempt to find a cure.
  - *Choice 4.2:* Seal the memory in the Codex of Regression OR purge it to maintain absolute emotional detachment.
- **Consequences:**
  - Establishes the core philosophical trajectory of the Future Self.
  - High `Tyrant Index` adjustments; sets `origin_acknowledged` flag.
- **Reveals:** The Future Self was once just as compassionate as the Past Self; the tyranny was forged through absolute despair, not ambition.
- **Transition:** As the past memory solidifies into historical fact, perspective shifts back to the Past Self facing the impending catastrophe.

---

## Act V — The Necessary Monster

- **Protagonist:** Past Self
- **Setting:** The Singularity Chamber (Point of No Return)
- **Narrative Objective:** Navigate the collapsing epicenter of the original breach, acquire the Singularity Core, and decide whether to accept the mantle of the future tyrant or defy the loop entirely.
- **Major Events:**
  - Reality within the chamber dissolves into non-Euclidean temporal geometry.
  - Phantoms of previous iterations appear, begging the protagonist either to save them or to let them rest.
  - The protagonist realizes that their own actions in this chamber will trigger the initial fracture.
- **Major Choices:**
  - *Choice 5.1:* Accept the temporal mantle, arming the Paradox Gauntlet and embracing the necessity of the Tyrant, OR reject the mantle, risking immediate timeline dissolution.
  - *Choice 5.2:* Sacrifice the facility's power grid to preserve Timeline Stability OR overload the core to pierce the barrier into the Atemporal Core.
- **Consequences:**
  - Locks ending eligibility paths.
  - Extreme deltas across `Tyrant Index`, `Timeline Stability`, and `Self-Trust`.
- **Reveals:** The Fracture was not an external event; it was caused by the Past Self's attempt to stop the Future Self. The loop is self-originating.
- **Transition:** The barrier collapses, merging past and future spacetime coordinates into the final convergence plane.

---

## Act VI — The Final Judgment

- **Protagonist:** Convergence (Past Self and Future Self in direct dialogue)
- **Setting:** The Atemporal Core / The Final Horizon
- **Narrative Objective:** Face the cumulative consequences of all decisions across Acts I–V and execute the final resolution for reality.
- **Major Events:**
  - The Past Self and Future Self confront each other directly across the final event horizon.
  - The complete Paradox Log is compiled and audited by the reality engine.
  - Final ending condition verification based on cumulative metrics and flags.
- **Major Choices:**
  - *Choice 6.1 (The Final Verdict):* Execute 1 of 5 philosophical determinations:
    1. Surrender to the Future Self's doctrine (**The Tyrant**).
    2. Erase both incarnations from the timeline (**The Sacrifice**).
    3. Overload the Singularity Core to restart the loop (**The Reset**).
    4. Shatter temporal containment completely (**The Fracture**).
    5. Achieve synthesis through total alignment (**The Reconciliation** - requires hidden conditions).
- **Consequences:**
  - Permanent resolution of the timeline.
  - Unlocks narrative epilogues and New Game+ memory records.
- **Reveals:** The ultimate truth behind human resilience and the limits of control.
- **Transition:** Game complete; credits roll; unlocks persistent Codex entries.
